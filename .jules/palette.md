## 2026-04-03 - [Documentation UX Improvement]
**Learning:** In sparse repositories with minimal files, establishing a root README.md and a non-circular navigation bar acts as a functional UI for documentation, significantly reducing contributor cognitive load and improving project discoverability.
**Action:** Always verify the existence of a central README.md and ensure cross-file documentation links are consistent and easy to navigate.
