# Palette Journal

## 2026-07-09 - Documentation-as-UI
**Learning:** In documentation-only repositories, UX is primarily a function of information architecture and reducing friction in critical communication paths.
**Action:** Prioritize pre-filled templates (like `mailto` or Issue Templates) to lower the cognitive load for contributors reporting sensitive information.
