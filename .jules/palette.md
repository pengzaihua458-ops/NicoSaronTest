# Palette Journal

## 2026-02-16 - [Documentation UX Patterns]

**Learning:** In repositories without a traditional UI, documentation acts as the primary interface. Improving navigation via "Quick Links" and streamlining interaction with pre-filled `mailto:` templates significantly reduces user friction and improves the overall experience.
**Action:** Always include a welcoming `README.md` with status badges and use structured `mailto:` templates for reporting to ensure consistency and ease of use.
