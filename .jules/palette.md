## 2026-08-16 - Vulnerability Reporting UX

**Learning:** In repositories with minimal UI/application code, security reporting processes often suffer from high friction due to plain email addresses without clear reporting templates or pre-filled mailto parameters.
**Action:** Enhance `SECURITY.md` with structured `mailto:` parameters (subject and body outline) and a copy-paste fallback template block to reduce user effort when reporting vulnerabilities.
