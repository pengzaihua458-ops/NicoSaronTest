## 2026-02-08 - [Streamlined Vulnerability Reporting UX]

**Learning:** In documentation-only repositories, vulnerability reporting represents the primary user interface. Reducing interaction cost via pre-filled email templates significantly improves the security reporting experience.
**Action:** Always provide structured `mailto:` links with pre-defined template parameters (`subject` and `body`) alongside clear, step-by-step copy-paste fallbacks to maximize usability and accessibility across mail clients.
