# Palette Journal

## 2026-02-08 - Reducing Friction in Documentation-Only Repositories
**Learning:** In documentation-only repositories lacking interactive frontends, user experience (UX) is primarily determined by information architecture. Reducing the cognitive and interaction cost of critical tasks—such as reporting vulnerabilities—via pre-filled structured mailto links acts as a significant micro-UX improvement.
**Action:** Always provide pre-formatted mailto links alongside clear, step-by-step fallback instructions to maintain a highly supportive and clear reporting experience.
