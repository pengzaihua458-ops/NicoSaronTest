# Palette Journal

## Palette Philosophy
- Users notice the little things
- Accessibility is not optional
- Every interaction should feel smooth
- Good UX is invisible - it just works

## 2026-07-13 - [Initial UX Assessment]
**Learning:** In documentation-centric repositories, UX is primarily a function of information architecture and structured communication. Reducing friction for critical tasks like security reporting is a high-impact micro-UX win.
**Action:** Prioritize structured templates for user-initiated communication (like mailto links) to ensure clarity and completeness of information.
