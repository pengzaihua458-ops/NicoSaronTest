## 2026-04-15 - [Documentation Hub and Navigation]

**Learning:** In sparse repositories, discoverability is a major UX hurdle. Establishing a central `README.md` and a standardized, emoji-enhanced navigation bar across all documentation significantly reduces cognitive load for developers and contributors.
**Action:** Always verify the existence of a root `README.md` and ensure cross-linking between security, performance, and UX journals using the standardized navigation pattern.
