# Palette's UX Journal

## 2026-07-15 - Micro-UX Reporting Template
**Learning:** In documentation-only repositories, UX is primarily driven by information architecture and reducing interaction cost. Replacing a flat email address with a structured mailto link reduces the user's cognitive and physical effort to report vulnerabilities while preserving accessibility.
**Action:** Always provide pre-filled mailto templates for security reporting in documentation-centric repositories to structure reporting and reduce friction.
