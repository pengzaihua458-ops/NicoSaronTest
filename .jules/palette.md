## 2026-06-30 - [Structured Vulnerability Reporting UX]
**Learning:** A structured `mailto` link with a pre-filled template (Subject: 'Security Vulnerability Report', Body: Type, Severity, Description, Steps, Impact) significantly reduces reporter friction and ensures high-quality initial reports.
**Action:** Always prefer structured reporting templates over simple email addresses for security and bug reporting flows.
