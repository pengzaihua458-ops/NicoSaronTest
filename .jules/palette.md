# Palette Journal

## 2026-07-25 - Reducing Interaction Cost in Documentation-Only Repositories
**Learning:** In documentation-centric repositories, user experience is primarily driven by clear structure, visual hierarchy, and ease of action (such as one-click mailto links). Reducing interaction cost for reporting vulnerabilities improves engagement and reporting quality.
**Action:** Implement structured mailto links with pre-filled subject and template body, combined with a copy-paste fallback block for users whose mail clients do not support structured mailto links.
