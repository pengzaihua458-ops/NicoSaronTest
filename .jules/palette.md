# Palette Journal

## 2026-07-15 - [Structured Vulnerability Reporting Mailto Link]
**Learning:** In documentation-centric repositories, micro-UX interactions can be optimized by providing structured mailto links with pre-filled templates. However, text fallbacks must be strictly preserved to remain highly accessible for screen-readers and quick skimming.
**Action:** Always provide both a pre-filled structured mailto link and explicit text-based field guidance to ensure a seamless, multi-modal reporting experience.
