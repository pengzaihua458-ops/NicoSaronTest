## 2026-02-08 - [Reducing Vulnerability Reporting Interaction Cost]

**Learning:** In documentation-centric or empty repositories, users reporting vulnerabilities experience high friction if they must manually compose emails with structured fields. Providing a pre-filled, template-driven mailto link significantly reduces reporting interaction cost and guides them to submit high-quality reports.
**Action:** Enhance security policies with template-filled mailto links alongside robust, easy-to-use copy-paste text fallback blocks.
