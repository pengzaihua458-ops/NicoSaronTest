## 2026-07-19 - Reducing Interaction Cost in Documentation-only Repositories
**Learning:** In documentation-only repositories, UX is primarily a function of information architecture and interaction efficiency. Pre-filling a structured vulnerability template directly within a `mailto` link significantly lowers the user's cognitive and physical effort, ensuring more structured and actionable security submissions.
**Action:** Always provide pre-filled mailto templates for manual/documentation security reporting channels to minimize interaction cost and prompt complete reports on the first attempt.
