# Palette Journal

## Philosophy
- Users notice the little things
- Accessibility is not optional
- Every interaction should feel smooth
- Good UX is invisible - it just works

## 2026-07-11 - Structured Communication as UX
**Learning:** In documentation-heavy or code-less repositories, "UX" is primarily about information architecture and reducing friction in communication. A pre-filled template for security reporting significantly lowers the barrier for researchers to provide high-quality data.
**Action:** Always look for opportunities to provide structured templates (mailto links, issue templates) when the primary user interaction is reporting or feedback.
