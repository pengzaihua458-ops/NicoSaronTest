## 2026-07-13 - Documentation-as-UI
**Learning:** In documentation-centric repositories, UX is primarily a function of information architecture and structured communication. Reducing friction for critical tasks like security reporting via pre-filled templates is a major micro-UX win.
**Action:** Always look for ways to reduce user effort in text-based interfaces by providing structured templates and clear calls to action.
