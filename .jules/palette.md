## 2026-02-08 - [Vulnerability Reporting Interaction Cost Reduction]

**Learning:** In repositories with limited interfaces or documentation-only structures, reducing the interaction cost for reporting vulnerabilities via structured, pre-filled mailto links drastically improves the user and researcher reporting experience.
**Action:** Always provide a pre-configured email reporting hyperlink alongside a clear, copy-pasteable fallback message template to lower the barrier to responsible security reporting.
