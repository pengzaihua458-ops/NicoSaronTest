## 2026-08-27 - [Streamlining Vulnerability Reporting UX in Documentation Repositories]

**Learning:** In documentation-centric or minimal repositories lacking interactive UI components, friction in security bug reporting can be reduced by providing structured `mailto` links pre-populated with subject/body templates alongside copy-paste fallback blocks.
**Action:** Enhance `SECURITY.md` with pre-filled mailto parameters and code-blocked reporting templates to ensure clear, effort-free reporting across all mail clients.
