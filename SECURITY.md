## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of our project seriously. If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, please report it to us by following these steps:

1. **Do not** open a public issue.
2. Email your findings to [security@example.com](mailto:security@example.com?subject=%F0%9F%9B%A1%EF%B8%8F%20Security%20Vulnerability%20Report&body=Dear%20Security%20Team%2C%0A%0AI%20believe%20I%20have%20found%20a%20security%20vulnerability%20in%20NicoSaronTest.%0A%0A%23%23%20Description%0A%5BProvide%20a%20detailed%20description%5D%0A%0A%23%23%20Steps%20to%20Reproduce%0A1.%20%0A2.%20%0A3.%20%0A%0A%23%23%20Impact%0A%5BWhat%20is%20the%20potential%20impact%3F%5D).
3. Provide a detailed description of the vulnerability, including steps to reproduce.

We will acknowledge receipt of your report and provide an estimated timeline for a fix. Thank you for helping keep this project secure!
