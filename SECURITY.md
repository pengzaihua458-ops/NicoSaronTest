## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of our project seriously. If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, please report it to us by following these steps:

1. **Do not** open a public issue.
2. Email your findings to [security@example.com](mailto:security@example.com?subject=%F0%9F%9B%A1%EF%B8%8F%20Vulnerability%20Report&body=Dear%20Security%20Team%2C%0A%0AI%20am%20reporting%20a%20security%20vulnerability.%0A%0A%2A%2A%F0%9F%92%A1%20Vulnerability%2A%2A%3A%20%0A%2A%2A%F0%9F%8E%AF%20Impact%2A%2A%3A%20%0A%2A%2A%E2%9C%85%20Steps%20to%20Reproduce%2A%2A%3A%20%0A%0A---%0AReported%20via%20Palette%20UX).
3. Provide a detailed description of the vulnerability, including steps to reproduce.

We will acknowledge receipt of your report and provide an estimated timeline for a fix. Thank you for helping keep this project secure!
