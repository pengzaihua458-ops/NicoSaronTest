## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of our project seriously. If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, please report it to us by following these steps:

1. **Do not** open a public issue.
2. Email your findings to [security@example.com](mailto:security@example.com?subject=%F0%9F%9B%A1%EF%B8%8F%20Vulnerability%20Report%3A%20%5BType%20of%20Vulnerability%5D&body=Hello%20Security%20Team%2C%0A%0AI%27ve%20discovered%20a%20potential%20vulnerability%20in%20the%20NicoSaronTest%20repository.%0A%0A%23%23%20Description%0A%5BDescribe%20the%20vulnerability%5D%0A%0A%23%23%20Steps%20to%20Reproduce%0A1.%20%5BStep%201%5D%0A2.%20%5BStep%202%5D%0A%0A%23%23%20Impact%0A%5BDescribe%20the%20impact%5D).
3. Provide a detailed description of the vulnerability, including steps to reproduce.

We will acknowledge receipt of your report and provide an estimated timeline for a fix. Thank you for helping keep this project secure!
