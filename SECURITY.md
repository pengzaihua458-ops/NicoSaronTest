## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of our project seriously. If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, please report it to us by following these steps:

1. **Do not** open a public issue.
2. Email your findings to us using the reporting template below.

### [Report a Vulnerability](mailto:security@example.com?subject=Security%20Vulnerability%20Report&body=###%20Description%0APlease%20provide%20a%20clear%20and%20concise%20description%20of%20the%20vulnerability.%0A%0A###%20Steps%20to%20Reproduce%0A1.%20%0A2.%20%0A3.%20%0A%0A###%20Expected%20Behavior%0A%0A###%20Actual%20Behavior%0A%0A###%20Impact%0A)

Alternatively, you can manually email [security@example.com](mailto:security@example.com) with the following details:

- **Description:** A detailed description of the vulnerability.
- **Steps to Reproduce:** Clear steps to help us reproduce the issue.
- **Impact:** What is the potential impact of this vulnerability?

We will acknowledge receipt of your report and provide an estimated timeline for a fix. Thank you for helping keep this project secure!
