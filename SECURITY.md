## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of our project seriously. If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, please report it to us by following these steps:

1. **Do not** open a public issue.
2. Email your findings using the template below:

### [Report a Vulnerability](mailto:security@example.com?subject=Security%20Vulnerability%20Report&body=Description%3A%0A%0ASteps%20to%20Reproduce%3A%0A1.%0A2.%0A3.%0A%0AExpected%20Behavior%3A%0A%0AActual%20Behavior%3A%0A%0AImpact%3A)

Alternatively, you can manually email [security@example.com](mailto:security@example.com) with a detailed description of the vulnerability, steps to reproduce, and its potential impact.

We will acknowledge receipt of your report and provide an estimated timeline for a fix. Thank you for helping keep this project secure!
